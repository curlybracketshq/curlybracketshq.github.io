Piano Trainer — Support

Sight-reading practice for iPad: read the notes, play them on your piano, get scored as you go.
Need help or have a question? Email giorgia.marenda@gmail.com — we usually reply within a couple of days.

Getting started

  • Put your iPad on the piano’s music desk, close enough that the microphone can hear the piano.
  • Tap Start mic and allow microphone access.
  • Tap play: after the count-in, play the highlighted notes — right hand on the treble staff, left hand on the bass staff.
  • Correct notes on time turn green; wrong notes or missed beats turn red or amber. Each hand has its own score.

Common questions

The app doesn’t react when I play.

Check that Start mic is on and that microphone access is allowed in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Piano Trainer. Play clearly, one note per hand at a time, and move the iPad closer to the piano if the room is noisy.

Which notes does it use?

C major, fixed five-finger positions: the right hand from C4 to G4 (thumb on middle C) and the left hand from E3 to B3 (thumb on the B below middle C). You can narrow each hand’s range and choose which note lengths appear in the controls.

It’s too fast.

Lower the tempo — anywhere from 40 to 120 BPM. The timing windows are deliberately generous.

Can I practise the same melody again?

Yes: tap Copy seed to keep a melody’s seed, then paste it and tap Load later.

Does it work with a digital piano or keyboard?

Yes, through its speakers — Piano Trainer listens with the microphone, so no cable or MIDI is needed.

Privacy

Piano Trainer collects no data: audio is analysed on your iPad and never recorded or sent. Read the full privacy policy.
