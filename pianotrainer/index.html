Read sheet music by playing it

A new melody appears on the grand staff, you play it on your own piano, and the app listens through your iPad’s microphone and marks each note correct or wrong as you play.

  • Treble staff for the right hand and bass staff for the left, eight bars per melody
  • The score scrolls to follow along and highlights the next note to play
  • Played notes are marked correct or wrong, with a separate score for each hand

Play on your own piano

Acoustic or digital piano, no cable or MIDI needed. The microphone hears what you play.

Practice at your pace

  • Tempo from 40 to 180 BPM, with a metronome click and count-in
  • Choose which note lengths appear, from whole notes to eighth notes, including dotted notes
  • Choose the note range for each hand
  • Every melody is generated fresh. Enter a seed to replay a melody exactly.
  • Three staff sizes, and controls that fold away to leave more room for the score
Private by design. Piano Trainer has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Audio is analysed on your iPad in real time and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.
