Privacy Policy — Piano Trainer

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Piano Trainer (“the app”) is an iPad sight-reading trainer: it shows a melody on a grand staff, you play it on your own piano, and the app listens through the microphone to score each note. This policy explains exactly what the app does — and does not do — with your data.

The short version: Piano Trainer does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Audio is analysed on your iPad in real time and immediately discarded. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. The app has no servers and makes no network connections.

Microphone

When you tap Start mic, Piano Trainer asks for microphone access so it can hear the notes you play. The audio is analysed on your device, a few milliseconds at a time, only to detect the pitch and timing of each note so it can be marked correct or incorrect. Audio is never recorded, saved to a file, or sent anywhere. Listening stops when you tap Stop mic or leave the app. You can turn microphone access off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Piano Trainer; the rest of the app keeps working without it.

Data stored on your device

None beyond what iPadOS keeps for every app. Melodies, scores and settings exist only while the app is running. If you use Copy seed, the melody’s seed number is placed on your clipboard at your request; the app never reads your clipboard.

Data sharing

None. Piano Trainer contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and does not share data with anyone.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports to the developer. This is controlled by Apple and by your iPad settings, not by Piano Trainer.

Children

Piano Trainer is suitable for all ages and does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
